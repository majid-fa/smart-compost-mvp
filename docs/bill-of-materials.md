## Before buying

- Confirm every voltage and maximum current.
- Confirm blower pressure is sufficient for air resistance through compost.
- Confirm CO2 range covers compost exhaust and the sensor stays in non-condensing gas.
- Confirm the load cell supports the vessel's maximum wet weight.
